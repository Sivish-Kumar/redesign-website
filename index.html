Roadmap

How µLearn Works

Four simple steps from opening your first task to building an undeniable portfolio.

01

Join a Chapter

Connect to your campus chapter (e.g. MBCET) or join statewide interest groups on Discord.

02

Form Learning Circles

Gather with 3–5 peers with shared technical goals to track progress and hold study check-ins.

03

Submit Micro-Tasks

Submit public proof of work (GitHub repos, blog writeups, demo links) to the task dropbox.

04

Earn Verified Karma

Accumulate points to level up, climb campus leaderboards, and unlock company hiring drives.
